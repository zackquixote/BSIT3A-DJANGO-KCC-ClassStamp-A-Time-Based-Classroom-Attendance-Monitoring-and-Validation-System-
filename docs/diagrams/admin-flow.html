KCC ClassStamp · Admin portal

What an administrator gates, from sign-in to term close

The admin console is not a settings panel: every branch below decides who can attend a class, or whether an attendance record survives. Two decisions are consequential enough to be called out — registration approval and the irreversible term close.

10 routes traced · 4 decision surfaces · 1 irreversible action

Decision surface 01

Registration approval

  • Gate between PENDING and APPROVED — an unapproved account cannot sign in.
  • Approval also mints the student's QR seed, so no badge exists before it.
  • Rejection requires a written note; both directions are audited.

Decision surface 02

Anomaly adjudication

  • The only path that can void an already-recorded stamp.
  • Rules arrive from the scan engine: replay, duplicate, rapid scan, off-window.

Irreversible

Term close

  • Promotion advances every regular student one year level.
  • No preview, no undo — the one action worth a confirmation gate.
